Title: Contract Length Selection - Customer Choice

Description:
Customer needs ability to select between different contract terms to find the best value for their situation.

Requirements:

- Contract Length Selection Interface
  - Given I am a customer selecting a broadband package
  - When I view the contract options
  - Then I can see 12 months and 24 months options clearly displayed
  - And the 24 month option is pre-selected as default
  - And I can click either option to change my selection
  - And the pricing updates immediately when I change contract length

Labels: broadband, contract-selection, customer-choice
Priority: Medium
Story Points: 3
